Questions, answered

What kind of video do I need?

A phone propped behind the baseline or on the sideline, 30 seconds to 10 minutes of play. We check your clip instantly and tell you if the angle or lighting needs adjusting — before it counts against your plan.

Does it work for doubles?

At launch we focus on your individual technique and positioning in any game, singles or doubles. Full doubles strategy analysis (stacking, who-takes-the-middle, partner spacing) is on the Elite roadmap.

Is this actually personalized, or generic tips?

Every fix is grounded in your footage — "you contacted 7 of 9 dinks at knee height, watch 2:14" — not recycled YouTube advice. If a report ever feels generic, tell us and we'll re-run it free.

What happens to my videos?

Private by default, always. Your footage is only used to generate your reports, you can delete everything with one tap, and we never post or share your video.
